## 1. No Telemetry or Data Collection
AutumnDeck is free and open-source desktop software. It does **not** collect, store, profile, or transmit any personally identifiable information, keystrokes, browsing history, device identifiers, or usage telemetry to the developer or any third party.

---

## 2. Network Activity
AutumnDeck operates entirely offline on your local machine, with the following single, optional exception:
- **Weather Widget:** When enabled by the user, AutumnDeck queries the public [Open-Meteo API](https://open-meteo.com) (`https://api.open-meteo.com`) to retrieve current temperature, weather conditions, and forecast data. No personal data is sent with this query. If geolocation is set to automatic, a coarse IP lookup via `ip-api.com` is used solely to determine your city coordinates.

AutumnDeck will **never** transfer any other information to networked systems unless specifically and deliberately requested by the user.

---

## 3. Local Storage
All user configuration, themes, widget layouts, and preferences are stored locally in your Windows user profile (`%APPDATA%\AutumnDeck\config.ini`). AutumnDeck does not upload your configuration to the cloud.
